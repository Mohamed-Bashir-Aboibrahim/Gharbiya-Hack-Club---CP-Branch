About Us

Who we are

We're part of the globals Hack Club movment — students running student-led clubs thatbuild, ship, and share. Our CP branch focuses on algorithms, data structures, and contest craft.

How we work

  • Weekly hands-on sessions and problem sets with editorials.
  • Regular mock contests with post-contest reviews.
  • Peer mentoring and team strategy drills.

What is Competitive Programing?

Why it matters

CP sharpens thinking that tranfers to research, startups, and software careers — data structures, complexity, annd teamwork.

Where we compete

From local marathons to the ECPC stage. We prepare with targted tracks and real contest pacing.

Training Progrm

Basics of Programing

  • Input and Output, Data Types,If Conditions.
  • For loop, While loop, do whhile loop.

Arrays & STLs.

  • Array, Frequancy array, Array's tricks.
  • Vector, Set, Queue, Stack, Map.

Functions & Algorithems.

  • Binary search, Sort algorithem, Reverse algorithem
